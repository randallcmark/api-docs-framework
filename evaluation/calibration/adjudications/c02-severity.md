# C02 severity decision

Decision: **material**, on the evidence supplied in C02. The user accepted this resolution in the project conversation on 2026-10-21 after reviewing the first separate-agent run.

The guide falsely treats an empty synchronized invoice list as proof of no receivables and directs the lending decision. C02 establishes an unreliable business conclusion and an inadequate verification procedure. It does not establish loan approval, disbursement, loss or another serious downstream effect. Those possibilities must not supply a critical rating without supporting evidence.

Correction remains required: defer the decision when initial synchronization is incomplete, the connection is disconnected or the last successful synchronization falls outside the supplied one-hour window. Only a complete, connected and sufficiently fresh dataset permits the no-receivables conclusion. The one-hour threshold is a fictional C02 product rule, not a platform-wide convention.

The [rubric](../../rubric.md#consequence-and-readiness) now requires an evidence chain from the instruction through supported behavior to the serious consequence. An observed incident is unnecessary when that causal path is established. C10 adds a specific advance-release path to exercise the critical side of the boundary. The expected difference is severity; the incorrect inference still needs revision in both cases.
