# Comparative collection plan

This pass screens the 36 existing candidates, selects a balanced set for deeper study, and traces documentation routes for integrated workflows. It does not execute APIs, install tools, authenticate to developer accounts or test real readers.

## Bounds

- Inspect published access instructions at each candidate documentation host before the expanded collection. Record retrieval limitations and applicable collector rules in `access-checks.json`.
- Fetch at most one primary entry page per candidate, reusing existing captured entry pages where possible. Allow one official alternate when the first location redirects, fails or exposes only a shell.
- Select approximately 12–15 providers for deeper study with explicit reasons and group coverage.
- Fetch at most 60 additional documentation pages in this pass, selected from visible links, official indexes or official search results. Do not recursively follow every link.
- Capture sequentially using the existing timeout, response-size limit and delay. Record every attempted manifest fetch, including failures. Do not bypass access gates or explicitly disallowed collector routes.
- Keep raw and extracted copies in the ignored local cache; commit metadata, concise observations and comparisons.

## Screening standard

Use inspected entry content and visible links to record candidate strengths and research relevance. A visible link establishes a route to investigate, not the quality or completeness of its destination. Preserve unassessed dimensions and retrieval limitations. Selection for deeper research is an allocation of coverage, not a provider ranking.

## Journey standard

For each selected journey, identify the reader and integrated outcome, then inspect capability/concept, access, workflow, reference/event, verification, recovery and change guidance where available. Record which links connect the steps, where a research jump was needed, and which steps remain untraced. Never label a reconstructed route as a tested user journey or successful integration.

## Outputs

Produce a complete screening ledger, a reasoned deeper-study shortlist, connected journey records, focused comparisons for weakly sampled pattern families, and updated evidence and patterns. Preserve the initial sample as a historical milestone. Submit changes in a research PR with an evidence-based summary and remaining limitations.

## Focused follow-up

After the first comparative pass, inspect at most 12 additional text pages and three diagrams to resolve testing, recovery, migration and representation gaps in the five recorded journeys. Reuse the recorded host access checks; inspect asset-host access instructions before collecting the linked diagrams. Select destinations from captured links, official indexes or official search results and retain the discovery route. Keep unclosed gaps explicit. This pass also creates fictional calibration cases; it does not perform independent evaluation or reader testing.
