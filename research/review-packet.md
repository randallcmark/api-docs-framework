# Comparative research review packet

## Outcome and scope

This pass makes the source pool broad and traceable, compares connected documentation routes, and turns the results into conditional discovery and evaluation guidance. All 36 candidates received entry/index screening; 15 are selected for focused comparison. Five documentation traces retain partial and untraced stages.

## Evidence and checks

Canonical JSON binds observations, screening decisions and trace nodes to captured body hashes. Markdown indexes are generated from that data. Full third-party captures and access-instruction snapshots remain in the ignored local cache.

The repository validator checks snapshot hashes, evidence locators, source/pattern/rubric references, screening completeness, trace capture bindings, visible source links and local document links. Isolated checks exercise broken references and missing-cache behavior. They do not certify documentation accuracy or reader outcomes.

API execution, account setup, tool installation, MDX compilation, independent scoring and reader-task testing were not performed. The user's evaluation platform remains untouched.

## Decisions and remaining work

No product-specific answers are needed to accept this research record. Benchmark journey selection and rubric calibration come after closing the verification, recovery, migration and representation gaps listed in the findings. Publication enforcement and numerical thresholds remain undecided.
