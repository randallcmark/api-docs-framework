# Plan a coherent documentation change

Inputs: discovery brief, pinned evidence, recorded owner answers, current site/page map and the relevant pattern-library entries. Reconcile consequential conflicts first. Approved intent alone cannot establish that a capability is deployed.

Propose the smallest coherent change that lets the stated reader assess, implement, verify and operate the outcome. For each affected page, state retain/revise/add/remove, reader purpose, evidence and dependencies. Choose concepts, guides, reference, troubleshooting or change communication by need; a guide does not require every available section. Reuse shared platform guidance rather than copying it.

Select applicable pattern IDs and explain each application. Keep them conditional. Consider endpoint semantics beyond schema, workflow completion, actors/tenant boundaries, synchronization, effective dates, sandbox differences and recovery only where relevant. Tooling guidance requires actual supported tools. A documentation correction does not automatically require a platform changelog entry.

Define acceptance as concrete reader decisions and observable results. Separate content checks, contract comparison, MDX build, safe example execution and reader tasks. Identify which checks exist and which are unavailable. Do not invent component syntax, simulator instructions, retries, idempotency lifetimes or guarantees.

Record outstanding questions and the parts of the plan they block. A plan is ready for drafting when the supported content and unresolved boundaries are explicit; it is not publication approval. Split unrelated reader outcomes rather than asking one reviewer to reconstruct several domains.

Return: bounded change plan, page/dependency map, applicable patterns, acceptance/check plan and remaining questions. Reuse the brief and answers instead of restating a long domain report.
