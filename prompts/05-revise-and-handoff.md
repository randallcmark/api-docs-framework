# Revise and hand off the change

Inputs: original separate evaluation, pinned content/evidence, owner answers and actual check results. Preserve the original evaluation. For each finding, record resolved with a content/evidence locator, disputed with rationale for a human decision, or still blocked with a focused owner question. Do not lower severity or change an evaluator's original score by editing their record.

Correct objective defects and unsupported claims. Obtain missing consequential answers before claiming readiness. Recheck affected criteria in a separate context when revisions or changed evidence warrant it; do not ask for broad repeated review after harmless edits. If no content changes are needed, record that outcome rather than manufacturing a revision.

Prepare the short PR packet: reader outcome and scope, evidence/brief links, consequential findings and their status, any decisions needed, checks performed with limits, and the specific review route. Include the actual diff. Keep historical outputs and long evidence outside the summary. Existing repository owners approve; this workflow has no automatic publication gate or overall passing score.

Record what was learned about question burden, ambiguity and avoidable rewrite demands. Distinguish measured facts from impressions; elapsed task duration is not active author/reviewer time. Preserve reusable product decisions and dependency links for later changes.
