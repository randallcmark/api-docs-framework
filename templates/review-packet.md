# Documentation PR review packet

## Reader outcome and scope

In a few sentences: who needs this, what they can decide or accomplish after the change, and which pages changed. Link the completed change brief for deeper context.

## Decisions needed

List only unresolved judgments that need a person. For each, state the question, owner, supporting evidence and consequence. Say explicitly when none remain. Do not represent silence as approval.

## Evidence and evaluation

Link the pinned contract/implementation evidence, approved product decisions, relevant patterns and actual changed MDX. Summarize critical/material findings and where the full evaluation lives. Keep unverified claims visible and distinguish resolved findings from outstanding ones.

## Checks performed

State the exact checks and results. Distinguish lint, link checks, contract comparisons, example execution and reader-task testing. An unrun check is unrun; an unavailable environment is not a pass. Link longer output rather than pasting it into the summary.

## Review route

Point the reviewer at the specific passages needing judgment. Record changes since the previous review and which earlier decisions were reused. Recommend readiness within the evidence scope; existing repository approval rules determine acceptance.
