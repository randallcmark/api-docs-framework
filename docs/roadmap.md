# Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Initial research pack

Capture agreed scope and research method. Index 36 candidates and inspect a bounded sample spanning payments, banking, HR, business software, and developer infrastructure. Demonstrate the chain from evidence to a pattern, a product question, a draft, and a review finding.

Acceptance: traceable sources; candidate, capture and review states remain distinct; actual local snapshots have hashes; provisional artifacts are labeled; repository checks pass.

## Milestone 2 — Comparative research

Screen the remaining candidate universe. Select approximately 12–15 providers with recorded reasons and study connected workflows. Prioritize the first sample's gaps: endpoint and field explanations, event contracts, business management, unified integrations, troubleshooting and support, release notes versus changelog, navigation and search, CLI and skills.

Acceptance: multiple independent examples per major pattern family; cross-domain and partner journeys; counterexamples; no platform endorsement inferred from visibility or brand.

## Milestone 3 — Calibrated guidance

Refine the pattern library, information architecture, page structures, product discovery prompts, and rubric. Produce scored practice examples at different quality levels. Resolve differences between evaluators and record examples and exceptions behind decisions.

Acceptance: evidence for criteria, shared interpretations of anchors, consequential failures not obscured by averages, and question burden measured.

## Milestone 4 — Repository workflow integration

Adapt the process to the chosen agent and MDX build system. Add actual spec/reference validation, examples checks, terminology checks, and PR reporting as needed. Existing repository checks and ownership rules remain authoritative.

Acceptance: a coherent documentation change can move through discovery, bounded drafting, independent evaluation, and a short review packet. Checks report their actual scope and do not imply semantic correctness from lint success.

## Milestone 5 — Evaluation and revision

Freeze a framework version, then evaluate the user's platform on existing-doc improvement and a new workflow or domain. Compare reader outcomes, accuracy, reviewer effort and author effort with a baseline. Preserve the initial holdout result and track later revisions separately.

Acceptance: observed results and remaining limitations, plus a prioritized implementation backlog. Reconsider tooling and product packaging based on measured needs.
